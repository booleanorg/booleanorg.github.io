About the group

A modest investment in a growing mathematical language.

L∃∀N is a formal verification language that makes mathematical definitions and proofs precise and machine-checkable. Our aim is not to become specialists immediately, but to acquire enough fluency to understand what formalization makes possible and where it may become useful in research.

Formal proofs

Express mathematical arguments in a language whose logical correctness can be checked by a proof assistant.

Research practice

Explore a tool increasingly relevant to formalized mathematics, large collaborative projects, and reproducible mathematical reasoning.

Mathematics & AI

Use formal verification as a natural complement to increasingly capable LLMs working on mathematical problems.

How we work

A seminar format, rather than a conventional course.

Weekly sessions

We meet for one hour each week, preferably at CUNEF, with the goal of maintaining a sustainable rhythm.

Rotating speakers

One member of the group guides the session, often by working through exercises from a section of the book.
